# V4-10.14 Footnotes

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10 Grounded Intent|V4-10 Grounded Intent]]

^footnotes

<sup>1</sup> **Affordance** (from the verb *to afford*): Gibson ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.13 Source References#^ref-gibson1979ecological|1979]]) coined the noun for what an environment offers an animal able to act on it, and the sense is relative to the agent. A handle affords a grasp only to a gripper whose aperture and wrench ceiling fit it, so the affordance record carries the gripper’s limits as well as the mug’s geometry.

^fn1

<sup>2</sup> **Special Euclidean Group**: “Euclidean” because its elements preserve distances, “special” because the rotation part has determinant $+1$, which excludes mirror reflections. A learned regressor carries no such constraint. An unnormalized quaternion or a matrix with determinant $-1$ is a valid tensor and an impossible pose, so admission rechecks quaternion normalization ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.09 The Intent Lease Specification and Contract#^sec-intent-lease|section 10.8]]) instead of trusting the output head.

^fn2

<sup>3</sup> **Symbol Grounding Problem**: Harnad ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.13 Source References#^ref-harnad1990symbol|1990]]) compared the problem to learning Chinese from a Chinese-to-Chinese dictionary alone, in which every definition points only to more undefined symbols. A model’s text embedding is that dictionary. Only a calibrated camera-to-base chain breaks the circle for the mug, so the proposal must carry its evidence record and not only its label.

^fn3

<sup>4</sup> **Hallucination**: In text generation the word names fluent output that the source does not support ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.13 Source References#^ref-maynez2020faithfulness|Maynez et al. 2020]]). For a robot the unsupported output is a coordinate, and nothing in its format marks it as unsupported. Containment therefore has to be a check on evidence that the model does not produce.

^fn4

<sup>5</sup> **STRIPS (Stanford Research Institute Problem Solver)**: The formalism was built to plan for Shakey, a physical mobile robot at SRI ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.13 Source References#^ref-nilsson1984shakey|Nilsson 1984]]), so symbolic planning began on a machine that moved. Its operators treat an action’s listed effects as certain, an assumption that slipping wheels and a misreading camera cannot grant. The gap that geometric samplers and motion predicates close is therefore as old as the formalism itself.

^fn5

<sup>6</sup> **Exchangeability**: A sequence of random variables is exchangeable when its joint probability distribution remains invariant under arbitrary permutations of sample order. Exchangeability is strictly weaker than assuming independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) observations, yet it suffices for the $1-{\epsilon}$ marginal coverage guarantee. Modifying station lighting or altering the conveyor layout introduces distribution shift that invalidates exchangeability, causing coverage guarantees to lapse silently. Calibration records must therefore be maintained on a per-station basis with mandatory expiration horizons, matching the lifecycle of target sensor evidence.

^fn6

<sup>7</sup> **Subsumption Architecture**: In the classic subsumption architecture of Brooks ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.13 Source References#^ref-brooks1986robust|1986]]), a higher layer subsumes a lower one by suppressing its inputs or inhibiting its outputs. Physical AI systems invert that priority. The lower, real-time layer retains absolute veto authority over deliberative proposals because its timing, stability, and failure behavior can be strictly bounded.

^fn7

<sup>8</sup> **Watchdog Timer**: An independently clocked down-counter that forces a hardware reset or a safe fallback output unless software reloads it before reaching zero. Because a crashed host cannot reload it, the lease countdown runs on the isolated Safety MCU rather than in the host process it supervises.

^fn8

<sup>9</sup> **Monotonic Clock**: A counter that only advances forward at a fixed nominal rate, such as POSIX `CLOCK_MONOTONIC` or a free-running microcontroller timer. A lease evaluated against wall time could gain or lose its entire remaining window in a single clock adjustment. Every measurement in this chapter is therefore evaluated on the Safety MCU’s monotonic counter and converted from external clocks with a bounded synchronization error.

^fn9

<sup>10</sup> **Workspace vs. Singularity**: Membership in ${\mathcal{W}}$ says that some joint configuration reaches the pose, not that the arm can move freely there; inside the workspace the Jacobian can still lose rank at a singular configuration, where some Cartesian velocities become unattainable. A workspace screen therefore rejects unreachable targets early, leaving singularities to the configuration and path checks; **[[05 Reference/Volume IV - Physical AI/V4 Control and Dynamics#^sec-appendix-control-kinematics|Forward kinematics and singularities]]** develops the Jacobian rank loss behind them.

^fn10

<sup>11</sup> **Approach-Speed Constant**: The standard’s speed describes a design person walking toward a safeguard, not a measured coworker, and the standard applies a higher constant to hand and arm reach at short separations ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/10 Grounded Intent/V4-10.13 Source References#^ref-iso13855|International Organization for Standardization 2024]]). A forecast built on the walking value is valid only for the motion it describes. A coworker who runs or reaches across the corridor lies outside that bound, and the growth term must therefore be validated for the site rather than copied from the standard.

^fn11
