# V4-08.14 Footnotes

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08 Sensor Perception|V4-08 Sensor Perception]]

^footnotes

<sup>1</sup> **Specific Force**: An accelerometer measures non-gravitational acceleration, ${\mathbf{a}}-{\mathbf{g}_0}$, so a unit resting on the floor reads one ${g}$ upward and one in free fall reads zero. Dead reckoning must therefore rotate specific force into the navigation frame and restore the gravity vector, and an attitude error ${\theta_{\text{att}}}$ leaks ${g}\sin{\theta_{\text{att}}}$ into horizontal acceleration, which integrates twice into the per-row poses that de-skewing relies on.

^fn1

<sup>2</sup> **Epipolar Line**: For a calibrated camera pair, a point seen in one image can appear in the other only along one line, the projection of the ray from the first camera’s center through that point ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.07 Spatial Error and Clearance Bounds#^sec-perception-error-clearance|section 8.6]] derives it). Stereo matchers search that line alone, so a rolling-shutter shear that moves a correspondence even one row off it produces a mismatch rather than a slightly worse match.

^fn2

<sup>3</sup> **Neuromorphic**: Carver Mead coined the term at Caltech for analog circuits that compute the way neural tissue does ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-mead1990|Mead 1990]]), and the silicon retina built in his group is the ancestor of the Dynamic Vision Sensor ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-lichtsteiner2008128|Lichtsteiner et al. 2008]]). Like the retina, each pixel reports change rather than level, so an obstacle that stands still in front of a stationary sensor produces no events and is invisible to it.

^fn3

<sup>4</sup> **Laser Class 1**: The class of IEC 60825-1 whose emission is safe under all reasonably foreseeable viewing, including through optical instruments ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-iec60825part1|International Electrotechnical Commission 2014]]). Below about $1{,}400\,\text{nm}$ the eye focuses the beam onto the retina, while at $1{,}550\,\text{nm}$ the cornea and lens absorb it first, so the permitted pulse energy is far higher. Those longer wavelengths need indium gallium arsenide detectors rather than silicon, which makes wavelength a range vs. cost choice made under a fixed safety ceiling.

^fn4

<sup>5</sup> **GelSight**: Adelson’s group at MIT turned a reflective elastomer skin and an internal camera into a contact sensor that recovers surface geometry and, from painted markers, shear force ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-yuan2017gelsight|Yuan et al. 2017]]). The design makes touch one more image stream, so a tactile frame carries an exposure, a readout, and a calibration of its own and must be dated and qualified like the navigation camera’s.

^fn5

<sup>6</sup> **ChArUco Board**: A chessboard whose white squares carry uniquely coded ArUco markers. The chessboard corners give subpixel accuracy and the codes identify which corners are in view, so calibration works from partial views of the board, the usual case for a camera on a moving wrist.

^fn6

<sup>7</sup> **Hand-Eye Calibration**: The problem was posed as ${\mathbf{AX}}={\mathbf{XB}}$ independently by Shiu and Ahmad ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-shiu1989calibration|1989]]) and Tsai and Lenz ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-tsai1989new|1989]]), for cameras bolted to robot wrists. The camera’s pose relative to the flange is never measured directly, only inferred from paired motions, so any error in ${\mathbf{X}}$ enters every grasp the arm plans from that camera, and poorly spread calibration motions leave it weakly determined.

^fn7

<sup>8</sup> **ROS Enhancement Proposal (REP)**: The Robot Operating System records its community conventions as numbered REPs, a process copied from Python’s enhancement proposals. REP 105 fixes the meaning of `map`, `odom`, and `base_link` and the continuity each must keep ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-meeussen2010rep105|Meeussen 2010]]), so a frame name written into a record denotes the same physical frame for every consumer that reads it.

^fn8

<sup>9</sup> **MIPI CSI-2 (Mobile Industry Processor Interface, Camera Serial Interface 2)**: The MIPI Alliance’s point-to-point camera link streams serialized pixels from the sensor straight to an application processor’s receiver and DMA controller, over differential D-PHY lanes or three-wire C-PHY trios, with no network stack between them. That directness is why the camera’s transport term in [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.05 Bounding Observation Age#^tbl-08-latency-budget|table 3]] is set by sensor mode and lane rate rather than by protocol processing.

^fn9

<sup>10</sup> **IOMMU (Input-Output Memory Management Unit)**: The device-side counterpart of the CPU’s MMU. It translates the addresses a DMA engine issues through page tables the kernel controls, so the ISP, CPU, and GPU can share one set of physical pages without copying while each device stays confined to its own mappings. A translation miss on that path is one more source of ingestion delay that the measured budget must include.

^fn10

<sup>11</sup> **Inverse-Depth Parametrization**: Civera et al. ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/08 Sensor Perception/V4-08.13 Source References#^ref-civera2008inverse|2008]]) introduced it for monocular SLAM, where a newly seen landmark may lie arbitrarily far away. Storing ${\rho} = 1/{Z_c}$ lets a point at infinity enter the estimate as ${\rho} = 0$ with finite variance, where a depth coordinate would need an infinite one, so near and distant landmarks share one well-conditioned representation. The full parameterization is given in **[[05 Reference/Volume IV - Physical AI/V4 Machine Learning Background#^sec-appendix-ml-spatial|Spatial Representations and Scene Parameterization]]**.

^fn11

<sup>12</sup> **Covariance Propagation**: First-order propagation maps measurement covariance to ${\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_c} = {\mathbf{J}_{\text{unproj}}}{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{\text{meas}}}{\mathbf{J}_{\text{unproj}}}^\top$ in the optical frame and then to ${\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{\text{body}}} = {\mathbf{R}_{bc}}{\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_c}{\mathbf{R}_{bc}}^\top + {\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{\text{ext}}}$ in the body frame, where the extrinsic term is what a mount shift adds. Its largest eigenvalue gives the principal variance scale, which for stereo points lies along the viewing ray; **[[05 Reference/Volume IV - Physical AI/V4 Systems and Hardware#^sec-appendix-systems-vision|Camera Projective Geometry and Covariance Propagation]]** derives the full propagation.

^fn12

<sup>13</sup> **Modulation Transfer Function (MTF)**: The fraction of scene contrast an imaging chain passes at each spatial frequency. A uniform blur of length ${L}$ in the image multiplies it by a sinc that falls to zero at a period of ${L}$, so detail finer than the blur is erased rather than dimmed, and no network downstream can restore what the exposure removed.

^fn13

<sup>14</sup> **Stereo Baseline Thermal Drift**: For a stereo baseline ${b} =$ 0.20 m and an unmodeled change $|{\delta} {b}| =$ 0.1 mm, first-order geometry gives $|{\delta} {Z_c}|/{Z_c}\approx|{\delta} {b}|/{b}$. At ${Z_c} =$ 20 m, the corresponding depth bias is about 10 mm, assuming focal length and disparity are otherwise unchanged. Temperature compensation must be checked against the actual mount and calibration.

^fn14
