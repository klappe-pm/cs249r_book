*Why can a safety check miss its deadline even when it runs on its own processor core?*

A correct refusal computed too late does not stop the motion it was meant to prevent. Separating a learned proposer from its permission path in software does not make the two independent while they still share memory, electrical power, clocks, or cooling. A burst of inference can then delay the check that should stop the machine, heat from the accelerator can slow the core that runs that check for seconds after the burst has ended, and a single supply or reset fault can silence both paths at once. None of these couplings appears on a software architecture diagram, and none shows up in average utilization, so a safety argument that has never been loaded on its real silicon rests on an untested premise. Moving the check to separate hardware removes some couplings and adds others, a communication delay and a longer chain of supplies and wiring, so no placement is independent by construction. Where each workload runs therefore decides whether the permission path can still refuse in time, and an action that crosses the causal boundary on a late refusal cannot be taken back.

**Learning Objectives**

- Conduct a dependent-failure inventory of the caches, memory controllers, interconnects, supply rails, clocks, resets, and thermal paths that each candidate placement leaves shared between the proposal and permission paths
- Explain why average bus utilization cannot bound a deadline, and identify which contention mechanism (bandwidth queueing, bank conflicts, or shared-cache miss handling) threatens a declared worst-case execution time
- Evaluate whether shared power and reset paths can defeat a selected permission placement
- Analyze thermal coupling and clock throttling hysteresis between multi-tenant workloads across disparate physical timescales
- Size a windowed watchdog’s service window from justified completion and inter-tick jitter bounds for a non-blocking record exchange
- Select among static reservation, spatial partitioning, workload reduction, and physical silicon relocation from measured interference and timing allowances, and allocate the dispatch-to-admission interval of an on-board or off-board proposer against the stopping budget
- Design an adversarial stress campaign whose falsification threshold is the permission path’s declared worst-case execution time (${T_{\text{wcet}}}$)
- Construct a placement record formalizing the deployment envelope that scopes loaded observed maxima, thermal headroom, bus arbitration, mitigations, and untested conditions for release review
