# V4-06.11 Summary

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/02 Teaching the Machine/06 Policy Training/V4-06 Policy Training|V4-06 Policy Training]]

^sec-training-summary

A policy’s weights keep what its training experience rewarded and forget which experience that was, so each regime is best remembered by the dependency it leaves behind. Demonstrations leave a dependency on coverage: a point-regression head averages valid modes into an action no one chose, and even a multimodal head drifts into states the demonstrations never recovered from. A reward-labeled log leaves a dependency on the support of the logged actions, and a pretrained base on someone else’s embodiment. Trial on the machine pays for its experience in calendar time, winding heat, and drivetrain life. Simulation pays almost nothing per trial but inherits every approximation in its contact solver and every mechanism it omits. Fine-tuning on the machine creates a new checkpoint that needs its own behavioral evidence and review of affected assurance premises. None of these dependencies shows in a validation loss or a simulated return.

Orthogonal to the source of experience, every policy must deliver its proposal inside the machine’s deadline ${T_{\text{deadline}}}$. The decoder that represents several modes buys that representation with dependent evaluations, and what decides whether it can run is renewal slack against the jitter allowance, not mean latency. When slack runs out, the chunk lease, not the decoder, bounds how long an admitted prefix keeps moving the arm.

The policy manifest gathers these dependencies into one claim with stated limits. Its declared ODD is the supported cells of the data join minus every state the omission log excludes, never a region read off a training curve. For the latch, that rule sends `latch-bc-01` to evaluation at the nominal plate and the guarded approach. It holds the simulation-refined checkpoints until paired probes bound the contact and timing omissions. It also lets `latch-bc-recovery-02` carry a wider claim that new recovery experience has to earn. An admitted action crossing the causal boundary carries no guarantee beyond the evidence that qualifies it, and the load-bearing relations behind that evidence are collected in [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/02 Teaching the Machine/06 Policy Training/V4-06.11 Summary#^tbl-06-key-equations|table 5]].

**Key Takeaways: Policy synthesis and sim-to-real transfer**

- **Every regime leaves a dependency no training score shows:** Offline loss and simulated return reward compliance with training data and numerical solvers rather than real-world physics. Candidate model rankings frequently invert when transferred to hardware. When an operating condition violates an unmodeled dependency, it produces a predictable failure signature that identifies exactly which runtime monitor the permission path must enforce.
- **Mode choice and recovery are separate problems:** Point regression can average valid, mutually exclusive demonstrations into an unviable intermediate action, whereas compounding execution drift pushes the system into states that the demonstrations never sampled. Multimodal decoders resolve action ambiguity; targeted recovery experience and closed-loop data resolve distributional drift. Independent runtime limits remain mandatory for both.
- **Decoder latency must fit the proposal contract:** Iterative denoising and deep autoregressive evaluations substantially increase compute latency and memory bandwidth pressure. When a policy cannot generate actions within the synchronous deadline, asynchronous action-chunk playback requires a validated hardware lease and deterministic fallback behavior. An expressive action distribution earns no extra execution time at the actuator.
- **Training information must survive the sensor boundary:** Privileged simulation states, such as ground-truth contact wrenches or global object poses, accelerate training, but the deployed student policy observes only noisy onboard sensor streams. Observation histories disambiguate physical variations only when those conditions leave distinct temporal signatures. Model distillation never removes the necessity for hardware calibration and empirical transfer testing.
- **Randomization covers parameters, not missing mechanisms:** Domain randomization widens policy robustness across modeled simulator parameters, but it cannot bridge physical effects that the numerical solver neglects. Backlash, unmodeled compliance, and sensor transport delays remain absent regardless of parameter range, requiring explicit entries in the omission log and channel-by-channel probe characterization on hardware.
- **Fine-tuning spends wear and reopens qualification:** On-robot trials consume component life, introduce thermal stress, and generate wear that cannot be parallelized across separate test rigs. Behavioral evidence gathered for an earlier checkpoint does not qualify an updated model, and independent permission properties require an integration check of their unchanged premises. A gated residual architecture strictly confines where learned adaptations take effect, but the integrated controller still demands thorough regression testing and hardware-level execution limits.
- **A wider declared ODD needs new support:** The policy manifest records dataset digests, training hyperparameters, and unmodeled simulator phenomena, explicitly excluding operational domains affected by unverified gaps. Scaling simulator throughput or pretraining a foundation model adds no supported cell to the declared ODD. Broadening the deployment boundary requires attributable physical recovery data and rigorous closed-loop hardware qualification.

^callout-takeaways--6-2-b89dc2c9

The declared ODD is where the second law, endogenous data (principle [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/02 Teaching the Machine/V4 Teaching the Machine#^pri-vol4-endogenous-drift|2]]), becomes a field in a record. A policy chooses its next observations by acting, so the states it meets in service are not the states its training visited, and no regime in this chapter escapes that. Cloning meets it as drift out of the demonstrated tube, learning by trial on the machine meets it as calendar time and wear spent visiting the policy’s own states, and simulation meets it as states whose dynamics the solver invented. The manifest does not remove the dependency. It writes down where the policy’s own evidence ends, so that everything beyond that line is treated as a claim still to be earned rather than as competence the weights happen to display.

When a candidate looks better than its evidence, the useful question is which of its states were visited on this machine and which were only scored. A wider declared ODD is bought with experience that joins to new cells, and choosing a training regime is choosing what that experience costs: an operator’s hours, a drivetrain’s life, or a probe campaign that closes a simulator’s omissions.
