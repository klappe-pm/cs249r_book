# V4-05.14 Summary

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/02 Teaching the Machine/05 Physical Data/V4-05 Physical Data|V4-05 Physical Data]]

^sec-data-summary

A physical machine manufactures its own training data, so every record carries the loop that produced it: the operator’s request, the permission path’s admission, and the body’s response, all under the machine’s current calibration and wear. Collecting that data is costly in a way scraping is not. In the latch campaign, only 24.6 percent of each scheduled hour survives as retained demonstration motion once startup, calibration, operator rest, resets, failed attempts, and quality filtering are paid for, and the wrist’s flex cable consumes the larger fraction of its assumed fatigue rating. Because every attempt spends cell time and component life whether or not it is kept, and because correlated control steps hold few independent samples, a campaign earns its cost by addressing a declared coverage, label-quality, or evidence deficit, including inadequate evidence within a populated cell.

Which states the archive holds is a question the archive cannot answer about itself. Coverage is a join between empirical occupancy and a scenario table derived from the declared operational design domain, and against the table the door actually demands, the baseline latch archive fills one cell of six. Inside even a populated cell, a cloned policy’s first error carries it off the demonstrated corridor into states where nothing in the archive constrains its output, which is why behavioral cloning’s worst-case cost grows with the square of the horizon. Interventions supply the missing learner-visited states, but only after curation at the divergence, takeover, and stabilization timestamps separates the learner’s commands from the expert’s recovery. Aggregating expert labels on those states makes the worst-case cost linear in the horizon only while the recoverability constant stays bounded; where a mistake cannot be undone, the state belongs to the permission path, not to the dataset.

A label is only as meaningful as the tap, clock, and plant behind it. Imitating the request teaches commands the permission path would have clamped, imitating the measured response teaches compliance and backlash as intent, and pairing either with a misdated frame converts timing error into position error at the speed of the motion. Range normalization cannot carry a label to another embodiment, and a bumped camera bracket or worn pad silently changes what a stored tuple means. The provenance record binds each archive to its taps, timestamps, plant, calibration, collection denominators, and known absences, so that training and evaluation can tell demonstrated experience from evaluated competence. **Key Takeaways: Physical data collection and provenance**

- **Coverage measures distinct physical configurations rather than sample volume:** Repeated nominal trajectories add millions of telemetry rows without expanding scenario coverage. Adjacent control steps are so strongly autocorrelated that an hour of permission-tick records holds only about 1,800 independent samples for a slowly varying statistic, and even independent samples support only the cells where they land. An engineering team must measure dataset completeness by joining logged trajectories against an explicit scenario table that spans the operational design domain, rather than relying on raw row counts or the archive’s own observed extremes.
- **Offline validation loss cannot detect coverage deficits:** Because conventional train-validation splits sample from the same underlying trajectory distribution, a policy can achieve near-zero validation loss while leaving critical operational cells unvisited. Only auditing the dataset against an independent scenario table reveals these silent omissions before deployment.
- **Every action tap represents a distinct physical control signal:** An operator’s commanded request, the resolved kinematic setpoint, the safety-filtered actuator command, and the measured joint response diverge whenever hardware limiters, friction, or permission guards intervene. Logging each tap alongside synchronized timestamps ensures the supervisory target remains causal and preserves unambiguous evidence of physical interventions.
- **Preserve aborted runs and interventions with rigorous causal attribution:** Discarding failed attempts discards the most valuable boundary-adjacent states in the operational envelope. Autonomous commands preceding a human takeover must be archived for fault analysis and counterfactual relabeling rather than misattributed as expert demonstrations, while the subsequent corrective maneuver should be reviewed, trimmed, and stored as an explicit recovery trajectory.
- **Physical telemetry remains bound to specific hardware embodiments:** While optical perspectives can be reprojected across camera geometries, dynamic properties such as joint friction, structural compliance, and gearbox backlash are embedded directly into recorded force and torque signals. Simple range normalization masks these physical discrepancies without resolving them, requiring thorough re-calibration and verification whenever data is transferred across hardware platforms.
- **Targeted recovery data broadens operational competence under new provenance:** An immutable dataset records the boundary conditions of a specific collection campaign, but engineers can systematically expand policy capabilities by appending verified recovery sequences under separate provenance tags. Asserting an expanded operational claim, however, requires end-to-end closed-loop validation on the physical machine under those target conditions.

^callout-takeaways--5-2-98d534a9

Each latch archive in this chapter ended the same way, with a short list of supported cells and a longer list of known absences. That ratio is not a defect of one campaign. It is what the second law, endogenous data (principle [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/02 Teaching the Machine/V4 Teaching the Machine#^pri-vol4-endogenous-drift|2]]), costs in practice. A machine whose actions choose its next states can only record the states its collecting policy chose, and every additional state it records is paid for in cell time and component life. The known absences in the provenance record are the price list. They say which experience the machine never bought, and therefore which claims no amount of training on this archive can support.

Read that way, the engineering question shifts from how much data a campaign collected to which absences it retired and what each one cost. The displaced-plate cell that `latch-recovery-02` fills moves from the absence list to the supported list under new provenance. The faster approach, which no one collected, stays on the list, and the permission path must keep refusing it until evidence arrives.
