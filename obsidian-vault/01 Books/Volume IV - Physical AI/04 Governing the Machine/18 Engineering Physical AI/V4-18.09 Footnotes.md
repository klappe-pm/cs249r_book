# V4-18.09 Footnotes

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/18 Engineering Physical AI/V4-18 Engineering Physical AI|V4-18 Engineering Physical AI]]

^footnotes

<sup>1</sup> **Amontons-Coulomb Friction**: The classical laws of sliding friction make the friction force proportional to the normal load and independent of the apparent contact area, which is what lets a single coefficient ${\mu}$ describe a contact. Bowden and Tabor ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/18 Engineering Physical AI/V4-18.08 Source References#^ref-bowden1950friction|1950]]) traced the law to the small true area of contact, whose shear strength sets ${\mu}$, and showed that a lubricant film only molecules thick can lower it several-fold. The coefficient therefore belongs to the pair of surfaces and whatever lies between them, not to the floor alone, and the braking premise needs a measurement at the tire-floor interface that no camera viewing the floor supplies.

^fn1

<sup>2</sup> **Popperian Falsification**: The asymmetry holds for universal hypotheses ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/18 Engineering Physical AI/V4-18.08 Source References#^ref-popper1959logic|Popper 1959]]). No finite run of safe stops proves that every aisle floor supplies the friction floor, and one measured film below it refutes that premise. A rate claim, such as a hazard rate per hour, is neither refuted by one incident nor proved by many incident-free hours, so it is bounded statistically and revised as exposure accumulates. This chapter therefore holds deterministic premises in register entries and rate claims in confidence bounds.

^fn2

<sup>3</sup> **Extremely Improbable Band**: The advisory circular offers its probability bands as aids to engineering judgment, expressed per flight hour of a mean-duration flight and applied to each failure condition separately, and it grounds such criteria in historical accident data ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/18 Engineering Physical AI/V4-18.08 Source References#^ref-faa1988ac251309|Federal Aviation Administration 1988]]). The $10^{-9}$ figure is therefore one catastrophic condition’s share of a certified design’s risk, not a rate for a whole aircraft, let alone for another kind of machine. Applying it elsewhere without the allocation argument that set its share leaves the number without its basis.

^fn3

<sup>4</sup> **Pooling Evidence Across Updates**: An updated model may alter the proposal distribution. Earlier data can be pooled only with a justified configuration-equivalence and exposure argument; an update does not automatically erase every observation. The exposure wall’s constant-rate model also requires a defensible treatment of shared fleet conditions and correlated faults.

^fn4

<sup>5</sup> **Auxiliary Signal Design**: The multimodel method computes a small test input that guarantees the normal model and each declared failure model produce different outputs within a fixed test horizon, despite bounded disturbances ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/18 Engineering Physical AI/V4-18.08 Source References#^ref-campbell2004auxiliary|Campbell and Nikoukhah 2004]]). A probe can therefore rule out only the failures someone wrote down as models, which is why the hidden premise must first be named in the register. The input bound and the horizon map onto this chapter’s budgets, since the probe must fit the force envelope and the separation must arrive before ${t_{\text{deadline}}}$.

^fn5

<sup>6</sup> **Proof Test**: Functional safety defines a proof test as a periodic test that reveals dangerous failures no online diagnostic can detect, so that the function can be restored to its as-new condition ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/18 Engineering Physical AI/V4-18.08 Source References#^ref-iec61508|International Electrotechnical Commission 2010]]). A hidden failure that arrives at a constant rate ${\lambda}$ is present, on average over a test interval ${T}$, for a fraction of about ${\lambda} {T}/2$ of the time when ${\lambda} {T}$ is small. The interval is therefore itself a safety parameter, because it bounds how long a weakened latch can go unseen.

^fn6

<sup>7</sup> **Fail-Passive and Fail-Operational**: Avionics separates a fail-passive function, whose failure causes no significant deviation of path or attitude and hands the task back to the crew, from a fail-operational one, which keeps performing through a failure. Removing torque from a wheeled base is fail-passive in this sense only after it reaches a qualified, mechanically restrained rest. A balancing biped in mid-stride has no such resting state, so its posture control must remain operational until the machine reaches a supported rest, and the certifiable reaction must be a controlled one.

^fn7
