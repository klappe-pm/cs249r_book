# V4-16.15 Footnotes

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16 Deployment Release|V4-16 Deployment Release]]

^footnotes

<sup>1</sup> **ISO/TS 15066 Values as Case Criteria**: The document supplies a body model and body-region force and pressure data for transient and quasi-static contact ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-isots15066|International Organization for Standardization 2016]]). ISO 10218-2:2025 incorporates the collaborative-application requirements formerly published as ISO/TS 15066; ISO/TS 15066:2016 itself remains published and is under revision ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-iso10218part2|International Organization for Standardization 2025b]]). The 50 N handover criterion is none of those tabulated values and not an injury boundary; the site assessment chooses it for one body region, contact type, and geometry, so a change to any of the three reopens the claim instead of inheriting its margin.

^fn1

<sup>2</sup> **Toulmin’s Argument Anatomy**: *The Uses of Argument* ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-toulmin1958uses|Toulmin 1958]]) splits practical reasoning into claim, data, warrant, backing, qualifier, and rebuttal. Safety cases preserve this anatomy: evidence supplies the data, stated confidence acts as the qualifier, and the rebuttal becomes the defeater evaluated during adversarial review ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.08 Adversarial Review#^sec-release-adversarial-review|section 16.7]]).

^fn2

<sup>3</sup> **Independence of Diverse Legs**: When independently written versions of a software specification were evaluated on identical inputs, they failed coincidentally far more frequently than independent failure models predicted, statistically rejecting the independence assumption ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-knight1986experimental|Knight and Leveson 1986]]). Design diversity does not by itself yield independence in failure.

^fn3

<sup>4</sup> **Conformity Assessment**: The formal procedure through which a manufacturer certifies that a machine fulfills all essential health and safety requirements before affixing the CE marking ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-eu2023machinery|European Parliament and Council of the European Union 2023]]). While manufacturers may self-assess conventional machinery, high-risk systems and self-evolving AI safety components require third-party evaluation by a notified body. Because the conformity assessment path is fixed before market entry, every record in the release manifest must be formatted for independent inspection.

^fn4

<sup>5</sup> **Safety Scanner Field Sets**: A safety laser scanner stores several protective-field geometries that were configured and validated in advance, and a safety controller selects one of them from encoded speed or position inputs. The scanner never computes a new field at runtime. `COND` borrows the pattern, so the verdict selects a pre-assessed limit set and no untested parameter can reach the permission path.

^fn5

<sup>6</sup> **Hardware Security Module (HSM)**: A tamper-resistant device can generate and retain a non-exportable private key and perform signing inside its own boundary. The signature identifies the signing key; authenticated access, sign-off records, and key-custody controls establish who authorized its use.

^fn6

<sup>7</sup> **Mechanical Fatigue Budget**: A cumulative-damage model such as Miner’s rule ($\sum_i {n}_i/{N}_i$) ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-miner1945cumulative|Miner 1945]]) can organize a measured load spectrum against component-specific fatigue data; it is an approximation, not a universal failure deadline. The assessed loads, uncertainty, inspection evidence, and stopping consequences determine any signed payload derating and cycle limit. An expired limit revokes the applicable release case rather than silently extending service.

^fn7

<sup>8</sup> **Chassis Dynamometer**: Powered rollers under the drive wheels absorb or supply torque, so the base can run at aisle speed on a bench while its drive and braking torques are measured. The rollers, not the floor, set the traction. Dynamometer stops can therefore qualify drive and brake response but say nothing about the friction premise of C3.

^fn8

<sup>9</sup> **Strain-Wave Gear Wear**: The arm’s joints use strain-wave reducers (**[[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/01 Machine Anatomy/02 The Physical Body/V4-02.05 Actuator Transmission Limits#^sec-body-transmission-compliance|Transmission compliance, contact impedance, and tooth fracture]]**), and in service their flexspline teeth and wave-generator bearing wear. Wear raises lost motion and torsional hysteresis, which adds phase lag between motor and link, so the arm begins to decelerate later and its first contact peak rises.

^fn9

<sup>10</sup> **Normalization of Deviance**: The sociologist Diane Vaughan coined the term in her study of the Challenger launch decision ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-vaughan1996challenger|Vaughan 1996]]). It names a process rather than a decision, in which each anomaly that caused no harm widened what the next review accepted as normal.

^fn10

<sup>11</sup> **Cryptographic Manifest and Silicon Attestation**: One-time programmable electronic fuses (OTP/eFuse) store the adjudicator’s public verification key in silicon, preventing unauthorized software updates from replacing the root of trust. Secure bootstrap validates each early boot stage before handing over execution control ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-arbaugh1997secure|Arbaugh et al. 1997]]), while measured boot extends cryptographic measurements into Trusted Platform Module Platform Configuration Registers (TPM PCRs). The hardware verifier checks these measurements against the digests specified in the signed manifest; any unsigned or modified binary image fails attestation and never receives an operational permit.

^fn11

<sup>12</sup> **Canary** (the miners’ sentinel bird): Miners carried caged canaries because the birds succumb to carbon monoxide before people do, so a bird’s distress warned the crew to leave. The metaphor carries this section’s warning. The canary is the unit exposed to the hazard first, so on a physical fleet its exposure must be bounded by containment, not by how few units are chosen.

^fn12

<sup>13</sup> **Non-Inferiority Testing**: The design comes from clinical trials, where failing to find a difference proves nothing, so a new treatment must instead be shown worse than the standard by no more than a margin fixed before any data arrive ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/04 Governing the Machine/16 Deployment Release/V4-16.14 Source References#^ref-blackwelder1982proving|Blackwelder 1982]]). A canary can therefore pass on modest exposure without proving the candidate better, but only if ${\delta}$ was not chosen after the result was seen.

^fn13
