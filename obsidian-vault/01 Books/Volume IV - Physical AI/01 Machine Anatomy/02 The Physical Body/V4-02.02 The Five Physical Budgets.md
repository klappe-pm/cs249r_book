# V4-02.02 The Five Physical Budgets

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/01 Machine Anatomy/02 The Physical Body/V4-02 The Physical Body|V4-02 The Physical Body]]

^sec-body-five-budgets

Every action a learned model proposes ultimately manifests as electrical current flowing through a motor winding, and from that instant physics governs execution. Machine learning practice rarely contends with this transition. Policies trained in simulation typically interact with idealized plants where commanded torque appears instantaneously, heat never accumulates in windings, and direct current (DC) bus voltages never sag under load. A physical machine honors none of these simplifications. The **physical body** is the coupled assembly of actuators, mechanical transmissions, structural linkages, power electronics, and sensors that translates digital commands into mechanical motion. Its physical dynamics bound what even a mathematically optimal policy can achieve. An emergency brake command requires finite time to develop mechanical retarding force and clearance distance to dissipate kinetic energy. Similarly, commanding a manipulator to hold a heavy payload motionless draws continuous phase current, dissipating resistive heat into the stator even though the joint does zero mechanical work.

The proposal boundary in **[[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/00 Introduction/01 The Causal Boundary/V4-01.08 The Machine in Five Levels#^sec-boundary-machine-model|The Machine in Five Levels]]** assigns the permission path responsibility for admitting action, and the body provides the concrete physical envelopes that make that gating decision meaningful. Before committing a command to hardware registers, the permission path must evaluate five physical budgets. It must confirm that the sensor observation remains fresh, that the moving machine can stop within available clearance, that the actuator can deliver the required torque trajectory, that the winding can tolerate the resulting thermal rise, and that the electrical bus can support the transient current demand. These mechanisms form a direct causal chain. Sensor and pipeline latencies consume allowable stopping distance. Arresting momentum demands delivered actuator torque. Generating that torque dissipates heat in motor coils and pulls current from a shared power rail. For any given maneuver, the governing systems question is which of these physical budgets binds first, under what operational stress, and on what empirical evidence.

1.  **Measurement freshness** (${t_{\text{age}}}$): The observation must remain valid until the mechanical plant responds. Latency accumulated across sensor exposure, data transport, neural inference, and low-level drive dispatch consumes allowable tracking margins as the physical world continues to move.
2.  **Kinetic momentum** (${E_k}, {p}$): A moving machine carries kinetic energy (${E_k} = \frac{1}{2} {m} {v}^2$) and linear or angular momentum (${p} = {m} {v}$). Bringing the plant to rest demands applying retarding force over finite distance and time; safe operating velocity must therefore preserve a stopping envelope well within available physical clearance.
3.  **Actuator bandwidth and reflected inertia** (${N_{\text{gear}}}^2 {J_{\text{rotor}}}$): Winding inductance and supply voltage limit the rate at which torque can change. A gearhead with reduction ratio ${N_{\text{gear}}}$ multiplies delivered torque by ${N_{\text{gear}}}$, but it also scales the motor rotor’s inertia ${J_{\text{rotor}}}$ by ${N_{\text{gear}}}^2$ as seen at the joint output ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/01 Machine Anatomy/02 The Physical Body/V4-02.05 Actuator Transmission Limits#^sec-body-reflected-rotor-inertia|section 2.4.3]]), which constrains instantaneous acceleration.
4.  **Thermal capacity** (${\hat{T}_{\text{wind}}}$): Resistive losses (${P_{\text{loss}}} = {i_q}^2 {R_{\text{eff}}}$, with ${R_{\text{eff}}}$ the winding’s effective loss resistance) deposit thermal energy directly into the stator windings and inverter switching stages. Heat dissipation limits sustained continuous torque, while the thermal mass of the coils constrains the magnitude, duration, and repetition rate of transient torque bursts.
5.  **Electrical power integrity** (${V_{\text{rail}}}$): Onboard compute clusters and motor inverters draw from a shared DC power rail. Peak motor accelerations must not drag the bus voltage below digital logic thresholds, while regenerative braking must provide an electrical sink so returned kinetic energy does not overvoltage the bus.

Under the **simultaneous admission invariant**, motion permission requires a non-negative margin in every budget before the permission path commits torque to hardware registers. Each margin must be checked in its own units; time, distance, torque, temperature, and voltage are separate constraints, not interchangeable quantities:

$$
\begin{aligned} {t_{\text{age}}} &\le {\Delta t_{\text{fresh}}}, \\ {d_{\text{stop}}} &\le {D_{\text{clear}}}, \\ |{\tau}| &\le {\tau_{\text{out,max}}}, \\ {\hat{T}_{\text{wind}}} &\le {T_{\text{limit}}}, \\ {V_{\text{rail,min}}} &\ge {V_{\text{uvlo}}}. \end{aligned}
$$

 Here ${\tau}$ is requested torque and ${\tau_{\text{out,max}}}$ is the maximum deliverable output torque under current electrical and thermal conditions. The estimated winding temperature ${\hat{T}_{\text{wind}}}$ must remain below its limit, while the lowest rail voltage ${V_{\text{rail,min}}}$ must remain above the undervoltage-lockout threshold ${V_{\text{uvlo}}}$ at which the supply regulator shuts down. The fifth line checks only undervoltage. Permission must also require regenerative braking to stay below the qualified upper operating-voltage limit of the supplied components, with an available absorption or dissipation path. Two lines state their budgets in derived form. The kinetic budget appears as the stopping distance it demands, ${d_{\text{stop}}} \le {D_{\text{clear}}}$. The actuator line checks only torque magnitude, because the slew-rate limit that winding inductance and reflected inertia impose enters the stopping line instead, through the brake-onset term of the pre-brake delay ${\tau_{\text{delay}}}$. All five inequalities must hold simultaneously. A surplus in four budgets cannot compensate for a deficit in the fifth. A fresh observation cannot prevent a stop from overrunning if kinetic energy exceeds available clearance, and abundant battery energy cannot prevent an actuator from burning out if thermal dissipation limits are violated.

**Systems Perspective 2.1: Why physical limits bound physical AI architecture**

The permission path requires concrete bounds on physical behavior, not abstract software promises. Accepting a torque command does not guarantee that the inverter can deliver the requested current against the back electromotive force (back-EMF) the spinning motor induces in its own windings, nor does cutting motor power guarantee that friction will arrest the plant before it breaches a clearance zone. The five physical budgets bridge this gap, tying software proposals to verifiable, empirical responses under declared operating conditions.

^callout-perspective-2-1-43418324

The warehouse mobile manipulator that this book follows draws on all five. Loaded to at most 368 kg and moving at its 1.5 m/s drive limit (illustrative; see the Reader Guide), it carries 414 J of kinetic energy through aisles it shares with people. Each budget establishes an empirical operating boundary on the assembled machine under declared thermal and electrical stress. Each ends the chapter as a limit record, the six-element record that **[[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/00 Introduction/01 The Causal Boundary/V4-01.12 The Architecture of This Book#^sec-boundary-book-architecture|The Architecture of This Book]]** sketched from the stopping notebook: a value, the conditions under which it holds, its uncertainty, the margin defended, a runtime detector, and the provenance of its evidence. The first budget is the age of the measurement on which every command rests, which runs from physical transduction, well before any software process receives the raw sensor data.
