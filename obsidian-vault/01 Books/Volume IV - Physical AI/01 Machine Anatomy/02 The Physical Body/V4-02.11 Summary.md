# V4-02.11 Summary

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/Volume 4 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/01 Machine Anatomy/02 The Physical Body/V4-02 The Physical Body|V4-02 The Physical Body]]

^sec-body-summary

Every motion plan in physical AI must ultimately satisfy hardware invariants. Five coupled budgets translate a high-level trajectory request into concrete engineering constraints: observation age, stopping distance, delivered torque, winding temperature, and supply voltage. Because these budgets are tightly coupled, no single limit can be evaluated in isolation. Consider the warehouse robot operating on a 48 V drive bus: while the sustained electrical draw clears the converter lockout even from a depleted battery pack, the stopping budget rests on fragile assumptions. The machine’s credible deceleration margin is narrow and holds only while the drive current limit stays at the motor’s peak. Furthermore, its traction model depends on dynamic wheel loading, and its anti-tip threshold lacks empirical center-of-mass validation. Isolating and sustaining the safety permission path on a dedicated capacitor-backed supply protects digital control availability during bus transients. Certifying the physical safety envelope, however, still requires verifying the actual drive power or passive braking dynamics available during an electrical fault.

To make these boundaries enforceable, a physical limit cannot be treated as a bare scalar. Instead, systems engineers structure each boundary as a limit record that documents valid operating conditions, measurement uncertainty, safety margins, runtime detection mechanisms, and calibration provenance. On the reference mobile manipulator traveling at its 1.5 m/s drive limit, mechanical stopping dynamics consume 742.5 mm of the 1.10 m clearance distance. That figure already includes the localization bound and protective clearance, so the remaining 357.5 mm is a design budget. It covers the delay terms the body does not own (observation age, chunk lease, permission tick, and bus cycle), stopping-profile shaping, and the tracking bound that later chapters add. These are the first three rows of the stopping budget, which **[[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/03 Running the Machine/12 Safety Enforcement/V4-12.04 Stopping Envelopes#^tbl-enforcement-stopping-budget|The warehouse mobile manipulator's stopping budget]]** completes term by term, with the chapter that prices each one. These analytical values guide initial system architecture, while physical characterization of the fully assembled robot establishes the empirical envelopes that production controllers enforce across the governing relations of all five budgets ([[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/01 Machine Anatomy/02 The Physical Body/V4-02.11 Summary#^tbl-02-key-equations|table 5]]).

**Key Takeaways: The physical body and invariant budgets**

- **Age begins at physical transduction**: The data freshness budget spans the entire timeline from physical photon or contact arrival to mechanical force delivery. Because stage latencies vary dynamically, an end-to-end tail percentile is never the simple sum of individual stage percentiles. Robust software must preserve hardware capture timestamps, account for downstream actuation lag, and reject any action proposal whose underlying sensory evidence will be stale when the actuator responds.
- **Speed sets the stopping envelope**: The clearance quadratic in speed determines an admissible velocity ceiling only while runtime state, stopping mechanics, and credible deceleration satisfy its mathematical premises. In turn, credible deceleration cannot exceed the strictest bound among tire traction, vehicle tip-over thresholds, and available motor braking torque.
- **Gearing trades torque for reflected inertia**: A transmission multiplies actuator torque linearly by the gear ratio ${N_{\text{gear}}}$, but reflects rotor inertia back to the joint by ${N_{\text{gear}}}^2$. Because physical motion also depends on current rise times, inverter voltage headroom, mechanical compliance, and joint friction, software acceptance of a setpoint command never guarantees physical trajectory tracking.
- **Thermal history limits available torque**: Resistive copper loss (${P_{\text{loss}}} = {i_q}^2 {R_{\text{eff}}}$) scales quadratically with current, and winding resistance increases as internal temperatures climb. Actuator burst schedules must therefore respect transient thermal peaks rather than relying on average power limits. Furthermore, reducing holding current to alleviate thermal strain requires mechanical brakes or counterbalancing mechanisms capable of supporting static gravity loads.
- **The supply rail is shared**: Motor inverters and digital compute share a common DC bus. Coincident acceleration demands induce inductive rail droop, rapidly depleting voltage headroom above converter undervoltage lockouts, while unclamped regenerative braking can elevate bus voltage and damage inverter stages. An isolated, capacitor-backed power domain preserves safety permission logic through transient brownouts. However, completing a controlled emergency stop still requires sufficient braking torque, energy absorption, and stopping clearance; otherwise, the machine must rely entirely on a qualified unpowered response.
- **A limit is a record, not a number**: An engineering threshold safely governs an execution decision only when coupled with its valid operating regime, measurement uncertainty, safety margin, fault detector, and calibration provenance. Moreover, these limits lapse as hardware degrades (rising thermal resistance, increasing battery internal impedance, transmission backlash wear), so they require continuous lifecycle monitoring and recalibration.

^callout-takeaways--2-2-2011bc7f

Every budget in this chapter ends the same way, as a number the permission path can compare against a proposal before that proposal becomes current in a winding. That comparison is how the first law, irreversibility (principle [[01 Books/Volume IV - Physical AI/01 Machine Anatomy/V4 The Machine Anatomy#^pri-vol4-irreversibility|1]]), enters the machine as engineering rather than as a warning. Work delivered across the causal boundary cannot be recalled, so each command must remain subject to refusal before it produces physical work. Refusing it takes a limit that states its value, the conditions under which it holds, how uncertain it is, and what detects its violation. In operation, each relevant limit record must support the proposed maneuver under its actual conditions. On the running machine, the electrical budget passes only its sustained screen, and the arm’s illustrative thermal model gives a burst lasting tens of seconds. The credible deceleration rests on peak drive current, a dry floor, and an unmeasured center of gravity, and every stopping calculation inherits those conditions. When a budget looks comfortable, the first question is which condition of its record the next maneuver could violate, and the second is whether a detector would notice before the stop is needed.
