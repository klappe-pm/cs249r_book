# V2-17.11 Footnotes

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume II - Scaling/Volume 2 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume II - Scaling/04 The Responsible Fleet/17 Conclusion/V2-17 Conclusion|V2-17 Conclusion]]

^footnotes

<sup>1</sup> **Ring AllReduce**: A collective that sends $2({N}-1){M}/{N}$ bytes per worker and receives the same amount for message size ${M}$, giving ${T_{\text{ring}}} \approx 2({N}-1){\alpha} + \frac{2({N}-1)}{{N}}\frac{{M}}{{\beta}}$ under the ${\alpha}$-${\beta}$ communication model, with ${\beta}$ denoting bandwidth in each full-duplex direction. The coefficient approaches 2$\times$ message volume per worker as ${N}$ grows, while remaining bandwidth-optimal for large messages.

^fn1

<sup>2</sup> **Tail latency**: The estimate is one minus the probability that every server answers within its own percentile, which under independence is the per-server probability raised to the number of servers; **[[01 Books/Volume II - Scaling/03 The Fleet in Production/10 Serving at Scale/V2-10.12 Load Balancing and Request Routing#^sec-inference-scale-load-balancing-request-routing-4997|Load Balancing and Request Routing]]** analyzes the tail effects it summarizes. Shared congestion correlates server delays, so a real fleet can sit above or below this figure, and the independence assumption has to be checked against measured traces.

^fn2

<sup>3</sup> **Lifecycle assessment**: **[[01 Books/Volume II - Scaling/04 The Responsible Fleet/15 Sustainable AI/V2-15.09 Hardware Lifecycle and E-Waste#^sec-sustainable-ai-hardware-lifecycle-environmental-assessment-66ee|Hardware Lifecycle and E-Waste]]** evaluates environmental impact across a system’s lifespan, including embodied carbon from hardware manufacturing. Embodied emissions can become a substantial share of total impact on clean grids or when hardware is lightly used.

^fn3
