# V3-16.16 Historical Perspective and Further Reading

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/Volume 3 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning|V3-16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning]]

^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-history

The core mechanism of this chapter is older than language models. Williams and Zipser named teacher forcing in 1989, training recurrent networks by feeding the recorded target back as the next input ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-williams1989learning|Williams and Zipser 1989]]). At about the same time, ALVINN learned road following as a supervised mapping from camera images to a steering direction ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-pomerleau1988alvinn|Pomerleau 1988]]), the template for what control researchers came to call behavioral cloning. Both generations met the same obstacle. A policy trained on recorded prefixes is run on its own, and its first error moves it into inputs the recording never showed. Ross and Bagnell turned that obstacle into a quadratic bound in 2010 ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-ross2010efficient|Ross and Bagnell 2010]]), and DAgger answered it the following year by labeling the states the learner itself visits ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-ross2011reduction|Ross et al. 2011]]). Sequence-generation research reached the same diagnosis independently, with scheduled sampling ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-bengio2015scheduledsampling|Bengio et al. 2015]]) and the name exposure bias ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-ranzato2016sequence|Ranzato et al. 2016]]).

Preference learning has a statistical root. Bradley and Terry’s 1952 model of paired comparisons ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-bradley1952rank|Bradley and Terry 1952]]) underlies the reward models that Christiano and colleagues fit to human comparisons of agent behavior ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-christiano2017deep|Christiano et al. 2017]]), and InstructGPT combined supervised demonstrations with such a reward model to align a language model ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-ouyang2022instructgpt|Ouyang et al. 2022]]). DPO then showed that the separate reward model and reinforcement learning stage could be folded into a classification loss on policy/reference log ratios ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-rafailov2023dpo|Rafailov et al. 2023]]), the form this chapter extends from responses to selected trajectory spans.

The cost of adaptation has its own lineage. Model compression ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-bucilua2006model|Buciluă et al. 2006]]) and knowledge distillation ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-hinton2015distilling|Hinton et al. 2015]]) made a large model’s behavior a training signal for a cheaper one, and context distillation applied the same idea to a prompt ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-askell2021general|Askell et al. 2021]]). Adapter modules ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-houlsby2019parameter|Houlsby et al. 2019]]), low-rank adaptation ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-hu2021lora|Hu et al. 2021]]), and quantized frozen bases ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-dettmers2023qlora|Dettmers et al. 2023]]) shrank what each adaptation stores and trains, while the systems accounting of mixed-precision training ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-micikevicius2017mixed|Micikevicius et al. 2018]]) and ZeRO ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-rajbhandari2020|Rajbhandari et al. 2020]]) made the per-parameter memory of that training explicit. Agent fine-tuning joined these threads once models emitted tool calls inside interleaved reasoning and action traces ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-yao2023react|Yao et al. 2023]]; [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-schick2023toolformer|Schick et al. 2023]]), and trajectories generated by stronger models became training data for smaller ones ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-chen2023fireact|Chen et al. 2023]]; [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-zeng2023agenttuning|Zeng et al. 2023]]). What changes for agents is the environment: a trajectory has two authors, which is why masking, serialization, and on-policy coverage become engineering obligations rather than details.

For further reading, six works trace this lineage from behavioral cloning to modern trajectory post-training:

- Ross, Gordon, and Bagnell’s DAgger paper ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-ross2011reduction|Ross et al. 2011]]) states both regret bounds of theorem [16.1](https://mlsysbook.ai/vol3/16_fine_tuning/16_fine_tuning.html#thrm-15-imitation-regret) and the reduction to online learning behind them; it is the shortest route to seeing why where error is measured matters more than how small it is.
- Rafailov and colleagues’ DPO paper ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-rafailov2023dpo|Rafailov et al. 2023]]) derives the preference loss from a reference-regularized objective, and reading the derivation shows exactly which assumptions a trajectory-span surrogate relaxes.
- Ouyang and colleagues’ InstructGPT paper ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-ouyang2022instructgpt|Ouyang et al. 2022]]) documents a complete demonstration-then-preference pipeline, including the human evaluations that compared the model after each stage.
- Hu and colleagues’ LoRA paper ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-hu2021lora|Hu et al. 2021]]) motivates low-rank updates and reports experiments on which matrices to adapt and at what rank, the knobs this chapter says to set by evaluation.
- Rajbhandari and colleagues’ ZeRO paper ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-rajbhandari2020|Rajbhandari et al. 2020]]) itemizes training memory by owner and precision, the same inventory discipline notebook [16.5](https://mlsysbook.ai/vol3/16_fine_tuning/16_fine_tuning.html#nbk-15-adapter-state-inventory) applies to an adapter run.
- Krell and colleagues’ packing paper ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.18 Source References#^ref-krell2021efficient|Krell et al. 2021]]) treats packing as an equivalence to prove rather than a throughput trick, the stance the chapter’s packed-versus-separate logit test takes.
