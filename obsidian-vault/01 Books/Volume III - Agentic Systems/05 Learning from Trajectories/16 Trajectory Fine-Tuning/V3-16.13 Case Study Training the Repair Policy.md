# V3-16.13 Case Study Training the Repair Policy

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/Volume 3 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning|V3-16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning]]

^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-synthesis

The recovery record that opened the chapter can now become a training artifact. The behavior to teach is narrow. When the runtime refuses an invalid tool proposal, the policy should inspect the schema, issue a corrected invocation that satisfies the interface, and end with a bounded report. Consider an illustrative supervised fine-tuning run that ingests `repair_demonstrations_v1` to produce candidate policy `trained_policy_v1`. That artifact is a set of weights, distinct from `ws_r20`, the workspace the demonstration produced.

Each of the three adaptation choices is settled by evidence the chapter has already developed. The objective is imitation because the record supplies a demonstrated correction, not an admissible comparison; `e2` and `e4` were produced under different information, so they cannot form a preference pair ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.09 Trajectory Preference Optimization#^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-dpo|section 16.8]]). The behavior source is the admitted recovery corpus. It covers the malformed-argument refusal the recording happened to contain, not the version conflict that [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.07 Exposure Bias#^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-exposure-bias|section 16.6]] showed a policy trained on it would meet. That uncovered state is therefore the first candidate for a labeling round or a fault-injected record, and the first evaluation of `trained_policy_v1` must include trials that reach it. The artifact is a full-parameter update, chosen for two reasons the chapter has developed. The repair policy is a single role, so none of the multi-role sharing that makes adapters attractive applies yet. The lesson is also a new recovery behavior, the kind for which a small rank is not known in advance to suffice, so the full update serves as the reference that any later adapter candidate must match. The choice has a price. On the hypothetical model of notebook [16.5](https://mlsysbook.ai/vol3/16_fine_tuning/16_fine_tuning.html#nbk-15-adapter-state-inventory), it keeps 128 GB of parameter state where an adapter run would keep 16.13 GB. A rank sweep whose adapter matches the full update’s paired acceptance, or a second role that must share the base, would flip the decision toward the adapter, which would then be a different candidate with its own evidence. Changing any one of the three choices produces a configuration that needs its own evidentiary trail.

[[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.13 Case Study Training the Repair Policy#^tbl-vol3-repair-training-manifest|Table 12]] details the formal manifest bindings for this candidate policy. In production workflows, recording version identifiers alone is insufficient; a complete training record must capture the exact artifact contents, serialization parameters, and optimization settings required to reproduce and audit the resulting model.

|:------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

The mask and the serialization follow directly from the earlier sections. `target_mask_v1` selects the policy-response spans of `e3` (the schema query), `e4` (the corrected tool call), and `e6` (the final report), each with its authentic end-of-turn token. The malformed proposal and its pre-dispatch refusal at `e2`, the schema returned to `e3`, and the check summary at `e5` carry ${m}_{t}=0$; they stay in the prefix as conditioning and receive no direct loss ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.04 Serializing Trajectories#^tbl-vol3-serialization-repair-spans|table 2]]). Selecting `e2` would teach the malformed call, and selecting the returned schema or `e5` would teach the policy to write tool results instead of requesting them. Whether `serialization_v1` may use one concatenated transcript or must serialize each selected invocation separately depends on whether the runtime compacted history between `e2` and `e6`. If it did not, each selected response’s prefix is a prefix of one transcript and a single example is faithful. The manifest records which case held, from the delivered-input manifests, rather than assuming it, because a record that crossed the watermark would need one example per invocation at up to $({H}+1)/2$ the stored tokens ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.04 Serializing Trajectories#^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-per-invocation|section 16.3.3]]). If the run packs these examples with others, block-diagonal attention and per-example label shifts keep each example’s context its own ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.06 Batching Variable-Length Trajectories#^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-batching|section 16.5]]).

The release gate for `trained_policy_v1` follows from the lesson it was meant to learn. Lower held-out loss on the repair corpus shows only that the objective was optimized. Promotion needs budgeted task acceptance on held-out repair tasks under the same runtime and fixtures as the incumbent, compared as paired trials, with ${C_{\text{eff}}}$ reported for both so that a slower but more reliable candidate is credited correctly. Because the lesson is “consult the delivered schema,” the most direct test is schema-following accuracy and parametric recall error on a renamed-argument set: a candidate whose PRE rises there may be relying on the recorded interface rather than the delivered schema. The gate also needs trials that reach the version-conflict state the corpus does not cover, and a retention suite for the agent’s other required tasks. A candidate missing any of these measurements is held, however its loss compares ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16.12 Evaluating a Fine-Tuned Agent#^sec-vol3-fine-tuning-benchmarking|section 16.11]]).

Even when the evaluations justify a staged release, the proposal boundary stays where it was. Fine-tuning makes the policy’s proposals more reliable without making the model an execution authority. The runtime keeps enforcing schema validation, least-privilege tool permissions, process isolation, and hard timeout budgets, and the agent’s final report remains a claim the harness checks against workspace artifacts. The manifest is what turns one admitted recovery into a reproducible, auditable policy update; the runtime is what keeps that update’s mistakes bounded.
