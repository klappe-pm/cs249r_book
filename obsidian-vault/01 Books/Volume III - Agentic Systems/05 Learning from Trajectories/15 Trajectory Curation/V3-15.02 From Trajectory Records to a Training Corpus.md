# V3-15.02 From Trajectory Records to a Training Corpus

[[Home|Home]] · [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/Volume 3 Overview|Volume Overview]] · [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15 Trajectory Curation|V3-15 Trajectory Curation]]

^sec-vol3-trajectory-curation-records-corpus-a90a

The evaluation harness of **[[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/04 The Agent Runtime/13 Agent Evaluation/V3-13 Agent Evaluation#^sec-vol3-evaluation|Agent Evaluation]]** and the operational record of **[[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/04 The Agent Runtime/14 Operating Agents/V3-14 Operating Agents#^sec-vol3-operations|Operating Agents]]** produced more than a release decision. While they judged the repair agent (**[[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/00 Introduction/01 Introduction/V3-01.05 The H·S·A Exposure Space#^sec-vol3-intro-lighthouses|Four lighthouse workloads]]**) over repeated attempts on the connection-pool repair task, they also accumulated a durable trajectory log. The log captures every trial in granular detail: the environment observations delivered to the model, the code edits it proposed, the invalid tool calls rejected by the runtime, the subsequent recovery steps, and the final verdict scored against the task acceptance criteria. Some runs passed cleanly, others failed outright, and several remain stalled in pending states because regression suites or accounting checks timed out. To an engineering team preparing the next iteration of the policy, these accumulated interaction logs look like the natural, abundant raw material for fine-tuning.

*Curation changes what the policy learns from its records and leaves every exposure where the runtime closes it.*

The machinery built so far, however, does not specify how to translate raw logs into training data. The acceptance contract defined in **[[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/04 The Agent Runtime/13 Agent Evaluation/V3-13.03 The Task Acceptance Contract#^sec-vol3-evaluation-contract|The Task Acceptance Contract]]** evaluates the final outcome of an entire trial rather than assigning credit to individual tokens or tool calls within it. Consequently, an accepted verdict alone cannot distinguish between an optimal tool call and an accidental stumble that the agent managed to survive. Imitating raw logs reproduces whatever they contain. If an agent wastes ten calls exploring invalid syntax before hitting the correct command, blind imitation trains the next model to repeat that exploratory churn. Furthermore, rigorous evaluation requires held-out task distributions that the training process never touches. If an engineer partitions data naively at the trace level, different attempts on the same underlying task fixture land on both sides of the train-test split, silently destroying evaluation integrity. Finally, because the agent generated these records under its own behavioral distribution, any filtering rule directly shapes what the subsequent policy will produce, inducing feedback dynamics across training generations.

Bridging the gap between raw execution logs and active model training requires a dedicated systems stage. **Trajectory curation** selects, labels, and transforms recorded interactions for a declared learning use while preserving the evidence and constraints that justify that use. Where evaluation establishes whether a trial satisfied its specification under fixed conditions, curation resolves a different operational question: which behaviors and tasks the learning process should absorb, which steps must be masked or rewritten, and how to preserve statistical isolation across splits. The end product of this pipeline is a versioned, reproducible corpus tailored to specific training consumers, accompanied by strict provenance records rather than an ad hoc collection of success flags. Transforming these admitted records into tokenized inputs and loss objectives becomes the downstream responsibility of **[[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning/V3-16 Trajectory Fine-Tuning#^sec-vol3-fine-tuning|Trajectory Fine-Tuning]]**.

This chapter follows the repair agent from diagnosis to a published corpus. A failure caused by a truncated observation is the case whose remedy lies outside the weights, and a recovery from a refused edit becomes the record carried all the way into a corpus. The stages run in the order the risks arise. Before anything is collected, the failure must be shown to belong in the weights ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.03 Capability Gap Diagnosis#^sec-vol3-flywheel-gap|section 15.2]]), and the evidence that will later judge the trained model must be fixed and protected ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.04 Task Fixture Design#^sec-vol3-flywheel-fixtures|section 15.3]], [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.05 Split Design#^sec-vol3-flywheel-evaluation|section 15.4]]). Collected records then pass admission checks specific to the learner that will consume them ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.06 Staged Verifier Cascades#^sec-vol3-flywheel-verification|section 15.5]]), recovery records are divided into context and targets ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.07 Recovery Demonstration Curation#^sec-vol3-flywheel-curation|section 15.6]]), and the feedback created when a policy generates its own data is bounded ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.08 The Self-Improvement Loop#^sec-vol3-trajectory-curation-self-improvement|section 15.7]]). The remaining sections keep the collector within its capacity ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.09 Collection Pipeline Architecture#^sec-vol3-flywheel-pipeline|section 15.8]]), bind every record to its lineage ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.10 Trajectory Provenance Tracking#^sec-vol3-flywheel-provenance|section 15.9]]), and carry one record through the whole chain ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/05 Learning from Trajectories/15 Trajectory Curation/V3-15.11 Case Study Curating the Repair Record#^sec-vol3-flywheel-synthesis|section 15.10]]). The first decision is triage, because a failure that does not belong to the model should never reach a corpus.
