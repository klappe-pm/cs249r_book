<sup>1</sup> **Goal Structuring Notation (GSN)**: Kelly and Weaver ([[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/07 Synthesis/20 Conclusion/V3-20.14 Source References#^ref-kelly2004goal|2004]]) presented a graphical notation for safety arguments that represents the claims being asserted, the strategies that decompose them, the operational context and assumptions under which they hold, and the empirical evidence that supports them. Structuring an argument explicitly uncovers claims lacking supporting evidence or unverified operational assumptions, which are the gaps that [[01 Books/Volume III - Agentic Systems/07 Synthesis/20 Conclusion/V3-20.09 Safety Cases Built on Evidence#^tbl-vol3-safety-case|table 7]] is designed to expose.
