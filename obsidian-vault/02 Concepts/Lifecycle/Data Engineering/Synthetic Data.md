# Synthetic Data

Training or evaluation examples generated by simulators, programs, or models rather than collected from the target environment.

## Conditional Relationships

These are the taxonomy's draft editorial relationships. Read each relationship with its condition; an outgoing link is not a prerequisite claim.

- Can introduce [[02 Concepts/Lifecycle/MLOps & Operations/Distribution Shift|Distribution Shift]]. **Condition:** Holds when the generator's output distribution differs from the deployment distribution or repeats the generator's errors.

- Supplies instruction data for [[02 Concepts/Lifecycle/Training & Adaptation/Supervised Fine Tuning|Supervised Fine Tuning]]. **Condition:** Applies when generated demonstrations are filtered for quality and diversity.

- Is an alternative to [[02 Concepts/Lifecycle/Data Engineering/Data Augmentation|Data Augmentation]]. **Condition:** Both enlarge training data; augmentation transforms real examples while synthetic data generates new ones.

- Is an alternative to [[02 Concepts/System Types/Embodied & Physical Systems/Teleoperation Data|Teleoperation Data]]. **Condition:** Simulated demonstrations are cheaper to scale but carry a sim-to-real gap that teleoperated demonstrations avoid.

- Can cause recursive distributional degradation [[02 Concepts/Lifecycle/Data Engineering/Model Collapse|Model Collapse]]. **Condition:** When successive generations train mainly on earlier models' outputs without retaining real data.
