# Risk Tiered AI Regulation

Law that classifies AI uses by risk level and imposes obligations, such as conformity assessment, documentation, and human oversight, scaled to that level.

## Conditional Relationships

These are the taxonomy's draft editorial relationships. Read each relationship with its condition; an outgoing link is not a prerequisite claim.

- Mandates for high-risk uses [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Responsible AI/Human Oversight|Human Oversight]]. **Condition:** Applies to systems classified high-risk under the governing regulation.

- Requires conformity assessment by [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Responsible AI/Algorithmic Audit|Algorithmic Audit]]. **Condition:** Applies to high-risk categories; obligations differ by role (provider, deployer).

- Requires [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Responsible AI/Algorithmic Impact Assessment|Algorithmic Impact Assessment]]. **Condition:** For deployers of high-risk systems in categories where a fundamental-rights impact assessment is required.

- Can mandate [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Responsible AI/Content Provenance|Content Provenance]]. **Condition:** Regulation requires machine-readable marking of synthetic content.

- Obliges providers of high-risk systems to perform [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Responsible AI/AI Incident Reporting|AI Incident Reporting]]. **Condition:** For serious incidents under the governing regulation, within its reporting deadlines.
