# Hardware Service Lifetime

The period a hardware asset stays in service before replacement, over which its embodied impacts and capital cost are amortized.

- Trades off against [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Energy Efficiency|Energy Efficiency]]. **Condition:** Keeping older hardware saves embodied emissions and capital but forgoes newer generations' work per joule.

- Lowers the annual rate of [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Electronic Waste|Electronic Waste]]. **Condition:** For a fleet of fixed size, shorter refresh cycles retire more equipment per year.

- Sets the amortization period in [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Total Cost of Ownership|Total Cost of Ownership]]. **Condition:** Capital cost enters TCO through amortization over the assumed service life.
