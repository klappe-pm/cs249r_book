# Security and Trust

Follow threat models, least authority, privacy, and safety evidence across deployed, agentic, and physical systems.

This curated navigation lens is an editorial association, not a claim of dependency or equivalence.

## Concepts

- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Threat Modeling|Threat Modeling]] — Specifying adversaries, assets, interfaces, capabilities, and failure consequences.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Least Privilege|Least Privilege]] — Granting each component or actor only the permissions its task requires, for only as long as it requires them.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Capability Based Authorization|Capability Based Authorization]] — Authorization in which holding an unforgeable, scoped reference grants a specific right that can be delegated with attenuated scope and later revoked.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Prompt Injection|Prompt Injection]] — Untrusted content attempting to redirect model-driven behavior across an instruction or authority boundary.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Privacy Requirements|Privacy Requirements]] — Requirements governing the collection, use, retention, and disclosure of information about people or records.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Differential Privacy|Differential Privacy]] — A parameterized bound on changes in output distributions under a specified neighboring-dataset relation.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Security & Privacy/Privacy Budget and Accounting|Privacy Budget and Accounting]] — A declared bound on cumulative privacy loss across releases involving overlapping individuals, tracked through composition accounting to constrain further releases.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Safety & Assurance/Safety Case|Safety Case]] — A structured, reviewable argument, with its evidence, assumptions, and defeaters, that a defined system is acceptably safe for a stated use within a stated operating domain.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Safety & Assurance/Functional Safety|Functional Safety]] — Absence of unreasonable risk from malfunctioning electrical or electronic systems, achieved through a standardized safety lifecycle such as IEC 61508 or ISO 26262.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Safety & Assurance/Safety Filter|Safety Filter]] — A runtime component that passes a proposed action when it is certified safe and otherwise replaces it with a minimally different safe action.
