# Energy and Cost

Compare energy, carbon, and cost accounting against the useful outcome delivered by a complete system.

This curated navigation lens is an editorial association, not a claim of dependency or equivalence.

## Concepts

- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Operating Energy|Operating Energy]] — Energy consumed by a specified system boundary over a defined workload or interval.
- [[02 Concepts/Foundations/Quantitative System Models/Data Movement Energy|Data Movement Energy]] — The energy cost of moving operands between storage levels, which grows with distance and typically exceeds the energy of the arithmetic performed on them.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Energy Efficiency|Energy Efficiency]] — Useful work completed per unit energy, such as inferences or tokens per joule, measured within a stated system boundary.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Energy per Inference|Energy per Inference]] — Energy consumed to process one input end to end on a device, the binding metric for battery-powered and energy-harvesting deployments.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Energy Measurement Methodology|Energy Measurement Methodology]] — The declared sensor sources, sampling rate, measurement boundary, and attribution rules by which a workload's energy is measured, from on-chip counters to facility meters.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Lifecycle Carbon|Lifecycle Carbon]] — Emissions attributed to operation and embodied system resources under declared accounting boundaries.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Operational Carbon|Operational Carbon]] — Emissions from electricity consumed while operating a system, computed from energy use and the carbon intensity of its supply.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Embodied Carbon|Embodied Carbon]] — Emissions from manufacturing, transporting, and disposing of hardware and facilities, attributed to a system independent of its operation.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Grid Carbon Intensity|Grid Carbon Intensity]] — Greenhouse-gas emissions per unit of electricity supplied, varying by region, time, and generation mix, in grams CO2-equivalent per kilowatt-hour.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Carbon Aware Scheduling|Carbon Aware Scheduling]] — Shifting flexible workloads in time or location toward periods and regions of lower grid carbon intensity, subject to deadlines and capacity.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Cost per Useful Outcome|Cost per Useful Outcome]] — Total relevant expenditure divided by outcomes meeting a specified acceptance criterion.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Cost per Token|Cost per Token]] — Serving or API expenditure divided by tokens processed or generated, often priced separately for input and output tokens.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Cost Attribution|Cost Attribution]] — Allocating shared infrastructure spend to teams, models, or workloads so that owners see and act on their costs.
- [[02 Concepts/Qualities/Sustainability & Cost/Data Transfer Cost|Data Transfer Cost]] — The money charged for moving data across network, zone, region, or provider boundaries, such as cloud egress fees, which can dominate the cost of distributing data, checkpoints, or responses.
