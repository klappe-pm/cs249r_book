# Theme Index

Cross-cutting themes connect explicitly selected concepts across the four volumes. Membership is a curated reading association.

## Themes

- [[03 Themes/Constraints Drive Architecture|Constraints Drive Architecture]] — Trace how workload requirements, resource budgets, and deployment limits shape model and system choices.
- [[03 Themes/Memory and Bandwidth|Memory and Bandwidth]] — Follow capacity, bandwidth, and data movement from hardware through training and generative inference.
- [[03 Themes/Latency and Throughput|Latency and Throughput]] — Compare response-time budgets, queueing, batching, and useful serving capacity under an explicit workload.
- [[03 Themes/Accuracy and Efficiency|Accuracy and Efficiency]] — Connect model quality to representation, compression, and the measured resource cost of a useful result.
- [[03 Themes/Scaling and Coordination|Scaling and Coordination]] — Follow partitioning and synchronization costs across accelerator fleets and multi-agent systems.
- [[03 Themes/Reliability and Recovery|Reliability and Recovery]] — Connect recovery state, failure boundaries, replay, and production rollback without conflating local recovery with undone external effects.
- [[03 Themes/Security and Trust|Security and Trust]] — Follow threat models, least authority, privacy, and safety evidence across deployed, agentic, and physical systems.
- [[03 Themes/Energy and Cost|Energy and Cost]] — Compare energy, carbon, and cost accounting against the useful outcome delivered by a complete system.
